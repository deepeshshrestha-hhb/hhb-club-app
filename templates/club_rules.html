Sitting Out & Latecomer — Quick Reference

Sitting Out & Latecomer Rules

TL;DR — quick reference

Court Wardens: Rukhsar, Jalal, Thomas, Deepesh, Sandip, Altamash

⏱ The Basics

🏸Games are played to 21 points; 11 min is roughly how long that takes, then a 2 min break — about 4–5 games/hour.
🔔Buzzer goes → leader wins on the spot. Tied? One tie-break point.
👥~20 players, 4 courts → 4 people sit out each game.

🔁 Sitting Out

✍️Sign the board on arrival — order = your queue position.
⬇️Most recent arrivals sit out first; once everyone's had a turn, it starts again from the bottom.
🔂Sat out? You're straight back in next game.
🔄Want out early? Swap directly with someone in the queue & tell a Court Warden.

Board Key

1,2,3…Games played (running count)
XSat out this game
🦺Only Court Wardens update the X's or numbers — keeps the board accurate and avoids chaos.

🐢 Late & Waiting for a Court?

👀Check the score on whichever court nearest the board is actively playing (usually Court 3 or 4 — not always both running).
≥10Either pair at 10+ (past halfway of 21) — genuinely late, no X (but bottom of the queue, so you'll sit out next anyway).
<10Both pairs under 10 — you missed most of it — counts as sat out (X).

🐢 Late but Got 4 to Start a Court?

≥5Either pair reaches 5+ at buzzer → finish your game to 11, counts as played. Everyone else waits (~2–3 min) for you before the next rotation starts for everyone.
<5Both pairs under 5 at buzzer → stop there. No X, no number — neutral.
